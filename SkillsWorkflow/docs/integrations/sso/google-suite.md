In this article, you'll learn how to integrate with Google Suite SSO. When you integrate Skills Workflow with Google Suite, you can:

* Control in Google Suite who has access to Skills Workflow.
* Enable your users to be automatically signed-in to Skills Workflow with their Google Suite accounts.

---
### Prerequisites

To get started, you need the following items:

* An Google Suite subscription.
* Skills Workflow single sign-on (SSO) enabled subscription.

:::note Currently it is only supported **SP** initiated SSO.
:::

### Installation

To access the Google panel and configure your custom SAML application, please follow the link: https://support.google.com/a/answer/6087519?hl=en

To download the Skills Workflow metadata for uploading to Google Suite, please access the following link: https://auth.skillsworkflow.com/saml2/metadata
