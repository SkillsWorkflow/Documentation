Favorites let you bookmark the documents and dashboards you access most often. Favorited items appear in the **search popup** and optionally in the **side menu**, giving you quick access without navigating through modules.

## Adding a Document to Favorites

There are several ways to mark a document as a favorite:

### From the Document Header

1. Open any document (Project, Job, Deliverable, etc.).
2. Right-click the document header area.
3. Select **Add as Favorite** from the context menu.

<figure>

![img](/img/university/favorites/add-favorite-header.png)
<figcaption>Adding a favorite from the document header context menu</figcaption>
</figure>

### From a Grid Row

1. In any list or grid view, look for the **bookmark icon** column on the left side of each row.
2. Hover over a row to reveal the bookmark icon.
3. Click the bookmark icon to toggle the favorite status.

<figure>

![img](/img/university/favorites/add-favorite-grid.png)
<figcaption>Bookmark icon on a grid row</figcaption>
</figure>

Alternatively, right-click any row and select **Add to Favorites** from the context menu.

### Visual Indicators

- A **solid bookmark** icon (filled, in pink/red) means the document is favorited.
- A **light bookmark** icon (outline) means it is not favorited.

## Removing a Favorite

To remove a document from your favorites:

- **From the document header** — right-click and select **Remove as Favorite**.
- **From a grid row** — click the solid bookmark icon to unfavorite, or right-click and select **Remove from Favorites**.

## Accessing Your Favorites

### Search Popup

1. Press **`Ctrl+S`** (Windows/Linux) or **`⌘+S`** (Mac) to open the search popup. You can also click the search icon in the top menu bar.
2. In the popup, click the **Favorites** tab to see all your favorited documents.

<figure>

![img](/img/university/favorites/search-popup-favorites-tab.png)
<figcaption>Favorites tab in the search popup</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Click on any item to navigate directly to that document.

The search popup has three tabs:

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| **Find** | Search across all documents |
| **Mine** | Documents assigned to you |
| **Favorites** | Your bookmarked documents |

### Side Menu

When enabled, your favorites also appear as a dedicated section in the left side menu, providing one-click access to your most-used documents.

<figure>

![img](/img/university/favorites/side-menu-favorites.png)
<figcaption>Favorites section in the side menu</figcaption>
</figure>

:::note
The Favorites section in the side menu must be enabled by your administrator in **System Settings > Favorites > Enable Favorites in Menu**.
<figure>

![img](/img/university/favorites/enable-favorites.png)
<figcaption>Enable Favorites in Menu</figcaption>
</figure>
:::

## What Can Be Favorited

You can favorite any document type available in the system, including:

- Projects
- Jobs
- Deliverables
- Estimates
- Bills
- Contracts
- Clients
- Requests
- Purchase Orders
- Dashboards and Workspaces

## Tips

- Use favorites to keep your most active projects and documents at your fingertips.
- The **`Ctrl+S` / `⌘+S`** shortcut is the fastest way to reach your favorites.
- Favorites are personal — only you can see your own favorites list.
- Favorites are stored on the server, so they follow you across devices and browsers.
