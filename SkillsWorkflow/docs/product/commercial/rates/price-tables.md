# Price Tables

A Price Table holds the prices used when work is quoted. It is its own area in the main menu, and a Fee must be associated with one.

## Availability

Opening Price Tables needs the **PriceTableNavigate** permission; viewing one needs **PriceTableRead**, creating one needs **PriceTableCreate**, and editing one needs **PriceTableWrite**.

## Working with a price table

A price table's own fields are its **Name**, **Company**, **Currency**, **Start Date**, **End Date** and **Stage**. The dates bound the period the table is valid for.

A price table also has **Info**, **Files** and **Feed** sections, and can carry workspaces.
