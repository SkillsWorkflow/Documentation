# Fees

In Skills Workflow this is called a Fee: the agreement with a client for a number of hours or deliverables, tracked against a Price Table. Many agencies would call the same record a contract — it registers the hours sold to a client, and tracks that number against the hours actually placed across the Projects created under it.

A Fee is associated with a specific client, and must be associated with a Price Table.

## Working with a Fee

A Fee's own fields are its Name, Owner, Start Date, End Date, and Stage. Its **Estimates** section holds the Estimates created against it, and its **Projects** section holds the Projects created against it — a Fee can create either directly.

A Fee also has **Files**, **Feed**, **History** and **User Fields** sections, like other documents in Skills Workflow.

## Availability

Opening Fees needs the **ContractNavigate** permission; viewing a Fee needs **ContractRead**, creating one needs **ContractCreate**, and editing one needs **ContractWrite**.
