**Overview** sums up where the estimate stands: what has been billed and ordered, what is still to bill or order, and the costs and margin behind it. Click **Overview** at the top left of the Quotes tab to open it, and **Editor** to go back.

![img-box-shadow](/img/quotes/10-overview.png)
<figcaption>The Overview of an estimate</figcaption>

## What it shows

- **Billed**: what has been billed and the share of the estimate it covers, what is still **To bill**, and the split between **Billed (external)** and **Billed (internal)**.
- **Ordered (external)**: the share of the external cost already ordered, what is **Still to order**, the **External cost** and what is **Ordered (internal)**.
- **Final margin**: the margin with its percentage, the **Total Income**, the **Total Cost** and the **Hours cost**.
- **To receive (BV + TIP)**: the volume discount (**BV**) and the supplier discount (**TIP**) to receive from suppliers.
- A table with one row per deliverable: its **Status** (**Billed**, **Partially billed** or **Not billed**), what is billed and still to bill, what is ordered and still to order, and its final margin.
- **Cost by supplier** and **Income and hours by deliverable** charts.

## Rules and behaviour

The Overview shows saved figures. Save your changes to include them, and use **Refresh** to read the figures again.

Figures you have no access to, such as bills, purchase orders or suppliers, are left out, and the Overview says so. When the queries behind the Overview are missing on your tenant, or you have no access to them, it shows that it isn't available.
