The **Quotes** tab of an estimate is where you build and price the quote: its deliverables and, inside each one, the services, expenses and resources behind its cost and sell. Totals and margins update as you type. Nothing is stored until you click **Save**.

![img-box-shadow](/img/quotes/01-editor.png)
<figcaption>The Quotes tab: deliverables on the left, one deliverable open with its lines, and the estimate summary on the right</figcaption>

## Opening the quote

Open an estimate and select the **Quotes** tab.

What you can see and change follows your permissions on the estimate. A column you have no access to is not shown, and an estimate that is blocked opens read-only.

## The layout

The grid has one row per deliverable. The arrow on a deliverable opens its lines, grouped in three sections:

- **Services**: third-party costs, bought from a supplier.
- **Expenses**: costs such as hotels, meals or travel.
- **Resources**: internal hours, by typology group or user.

Each section header opens or closes that section. **Expand all** and **Collapse all** open or close every deliverable at once.

**Editor** and **Overview** switch between the grid and a summary of what has been billed and ordered. See [Overview](/docs/product/commercial/estimates/quotes/overview).

The **Estimate summary** on the right shows the totals, the final margin against its target, and a **Check before sending** list. See [Prices and margins](/docs/product/commercial/estimates/quotes/prices-and-margins).

When the estimate has several versions, the version buttons open the older ones, read-only.

## The toolbar

The buttons at the top of the estimate act on the whole quote:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Stores every change |
| **Undo the changes** | Discards every change since the last save |
| **Approve all** / **Disapprove all** | Approves or disapproves every line |
| **Apply table rates** | Re-prices the resource lines from the rate card |
| **Export to Excel** / **Import from Excel** | Work on the quote in a spreadsheet |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the quote |

Without permission to edit the quote, only **Export to Excel** and **Refresh** are shown. **Apply table rates** also needs a **Rate Card** and a **Rate Card Column** on the estimate, and permission to change rate cards.

## Help in the page

The **?** button opens **Help**, with the keyboard shortcuts. **Open the documentation** opens these pages in a new tab. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/product/commercial/estimates/quotes/keyboard-shortcuts).
