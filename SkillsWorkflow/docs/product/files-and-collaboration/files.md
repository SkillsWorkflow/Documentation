# Files

Files is its own area in the main menu, backed by a file system of its own. It sits alongside — and is separate from — the **Files** tab that individual documents such as projects, jobs and bills carry.

## Availability

Opening Files needs the **FilesNavigate** permission. Viewing needs **FilesRead**, creating needs **FilesCreate**, editing needs **FilesWrite**, and removing needs **FilesDelete**.

Files is one of the areas that has no list definition of its own: the area is reached directly from the main menu.
