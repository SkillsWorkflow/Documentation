# Planning & Scheduling

Once the work exists, it has to be scheduled and staffed. The Gantt puts a project's jobs on a timeline and sets the dependencies between them. The resource scheduler assigns people to that work and shows who is over- or under-loaded. Planned time records how many hours each person is expected to spend.
