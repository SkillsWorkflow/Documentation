# Bills

A Bill is the billing document raised against a client. It is its own area in the main menu, and it moves through the stages configured for its workflow like the other documents in Skills Workflow.

## Availability

Opening Bills needs the **BillNavigate** permission; viewing a bill needs **BillRead**, creating one needs **BillCreate**, and editing one needs **BillWrite**.

## Working with a bill

A bill's own fields are its **Name**, **Date**, **Management Date**, **Due** and **Stage**.

A bill also has **Info**, **Files** and **Feed** sections, and can carry workspaces, like other documents in Skills Workflow.
