# Errors and common questions

Look for the message you saw or the situation you are in, and follow the link to the steps that solve it.

## Estimates and invoicing

### "There must be at least one condition with billing value / percentage"

This appears when you invoice an estimate. The usual cause is a deliverable that is not approved, or a billing condition with no amount. [What to check](/docs/product/commercial/estimates/invoice-estimate#error-billing-condition-value-percentage)

### The services are approved but the estimate does not invoice

Services are created approved, deliverables are not. [Approve deliverables before invoicing](/docs/product/commercial/estimates/invoice-estimate#approve-deliverables)

## Purchase orders

### I corrected a service in the estimate and the purchase order still shows the old text

Purchase orders keep the text they had when they were created. [Changes to the estimate after buying](/docs/product/billing-and-costs/billing/purchase-orders#changes-after-buying)

## Bills

### I corrected the estimate and the bill still shows the old description

Bills keep the text they had when they were created. [Changes to the estimate after invoicing](/docs/product/billing-and-costs/billing/bills#changes-after-invoicing)
