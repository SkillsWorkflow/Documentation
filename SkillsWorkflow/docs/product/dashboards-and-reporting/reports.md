# Reports

Reports is its own area in the main menu, reached at `reports`.

## Availability

Opening Reports needs the **ReportNavigate** permission; viewing a report needs **ReportRead**, creating one needs **ReportCreate**, and editing one needs **ReportWrite**.

Like Files, Reports has no list definition of its own — the area is reached directly from the main menu.
