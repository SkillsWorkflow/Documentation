## Overview

Extract relevant information from uploaded files, such as briefs, spreadsheets, or project documentation, and use it to prepare an estimate.

:::info[What to ask]
Create an estimate for [Client / Project] using the attached files. Extract the project scope, deliverables, required resources, estimated hours, rates, costs, and any relevant conditions. Organize the information into an estimate with a breakdown of the deliverables, resources, quantities, rates, and total amounts. Highlight any missing information or assumptions that require confirmation.
:::

:::tip[What you get back]
A structured estimate generated from the attached files, containing the extracted project scope, deliverables, resources, estimated hours, rates, costs, and total amounts, with missing information and assumptions flagged for confirmation.
:::
