## Overview

Generate an estimate based on user-provided instructions, including the required scope, deliverables, resources, rates, and other relevant information.

:::info[What to ask]
Create an estimate for [Client / Project] based on the following instructions:

[Describe the project scope, deliverables, required resources, estimated hours, applicable rates, costs, and any relevant conditions.]

Prepare the estimate with a breakdown of the deliverables, resources, quantities, rates, and total amounts. Highlight any missing information or assumptions that need confirmation before finalizing it.
:::

:::tip[What you get back]
A structured estimate based on the provided instructions, including deliverables, resources, quantities, rates, and total amounts, with missing information and assumptions clearly identified for confirmation.
:::
