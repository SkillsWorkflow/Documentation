## Overview

Identify the most suitable dashboard or portal for sharing project information with clients, based on the information they need to access.

:::info[What to ask]
What is the best dashboard or portal to share project information with [Client]? I need a view that provides visibility into project status, progress, risks, contract burn, and pending requests. Identify the most relevant option and explain what information it provides.
:::

:::tip[What you get back]
The most relevant dashboard or portal for sharing project information with the client, including the information it provides about status, progress, risks, contract burn, and pending requests, with a link where available.
:::
