## Overview

Analyze contract consumption by comparing contracted values against timesheet records, workloads, costs, income, and time. Identify discrepancies, consumption trends, and potential overruns.

:::info[What to ask]
Analyze the contract burn for [Client / Project]. Compare the contracted hours and amounts against the actual timesheet records, workloads, costs, and income. Include the percentage of contract consumption, remaining budget and hours, and any discrepancies or potential overruns. Highlight the main findings and areas requiring attention.
:::

:::tip[What you get back]
A consolidated contract burn analysis showing contracted versus actual consumption, remaining hours and budget, costs versus income, and workload information. The results highlight consumption trends, discrepancies, and potential overruns requiring attention.
:::
