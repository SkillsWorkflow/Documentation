## Overview

Identify current or upcoming resource shortages across projects. Highlight resource gaps, affected projects, and periods where additional capacity may be required.

## Example prompt

```
Identify resource shortages across current and upcoming projects during [Period]. Highlight the projects, roles, or typology groups affected, the periods of insufficient capacity, and the gap between required and available resources. Identify the areas where additional resources may be needed.
```

## Expected outcome

A list of current and upcoming resource shortages, identifying affected projects, roles, and typology groups, together with the periods of insufficient capacity and the additional resources required.
