## Overview

Provide an overview of client profitability by analyzing costs, income, margins, and relevant financial indicators across projects.

:::info[What to ask]
Analyze the profitability of [Client / Client List] during [Period]. Compare project income against costs and identify the resulting profitability and margins. Highlight the most and least profitable projects, significant cost deviations, and any financial risks or opportunities.
:::

:::tip[What you get back]
A client profitability analysis showing income, costs, profitability, and margins for the selected period, identifying the most and least profitable projects, cost deviations, and potential financial risks.
:::
