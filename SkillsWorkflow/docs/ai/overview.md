Every day, your team turns a client email into a job, a rough note into a brief, and a brief into approved, on-brand work — over and over, across every account. Skills Workflow's AI is built into that exact workflow, so the busywork gets faster without adding another tool, another login, or another thing to learn.

No prompt engineering. No separate AI app. Just plain-language requests, right where your team already works.

## What your team gets today

### Spin up jobs and deliverables in seconds
Turn a client request into a fully-formed job, deliverable, or project — created, updated, duplicated, or moved to the right stage, just by describing what you need. No re-keying the same request into three screens.
→ [Document Assistant](agents/document-agent.md)

### Never stare at a blank brief again
Compose, rewrite, shorten, or restructure text right inside the brief and feed editors your team already uses — including turning a rough brain-dump into a brief that follows your job type's own template.
→ [Writing Assistant](agents/writing-agent.md) · [Writing Tools](/docs/university/ai/writing-tools)

### Connect external AI agents where you need them
Skills Workflow can plug in specialized external LLMs and AI agents — for example, one focused on structuring and validating marketing and campaign briefs — so nothing goes into production missing an objective, an audience, or a required section.

### Change how work moves without opening a ticket
Review or reshape stages, transitions, and approval paths conversationally — see how a document type moves through your workflow today, then adjust it.
→ [Workflow Assistant](agents/workflow-agent.md)

### Make every dashboard fit how your agency works
From a quick panel or filter tweak, to building a workspace from scratch, to full code-level control for your studio's power users.
→ [Workspace Assistant](agents/workspace-agent.md) · [Workspace Studio](agents/workspace-studio.md)

## Built into the platform, not bolted on

- Ask in plain language — your account managers and producers don't need to learn prompt syntax.
- Every assistant respects the workflow rules, templates, and permissions already configured in Skills Workflow.
- Nothing new to install or log into — it's the same jobs, deliverables, and workspaces your team already lives in.
