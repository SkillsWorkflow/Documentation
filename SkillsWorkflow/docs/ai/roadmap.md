Skills Workflow AI keeps growing. Here is where we are taking it — organized around four areas of your agency's day.

:::note
This roadmap is directional and subject to change. Everything under [Use Cases](use-cases.md) is available today.
:::

## Data

Know what is going on without going looking for it.

- Ask any data point in the system and get a straight answer back.
- Client status and sentiment at a glance, so you walk into every conversation prepared.
- Proactive alerts on what matters — margins, overheads, an overloaded team — before you have to ask.

## Processes

Take the guesswork out of how work moves.

- AI that explains how a process is set up and suggests the sensible next step.
- Summarize long discussions and attached files into what actually happened.
- Auto-draft descriptions so nothing starts from a blank page.

## Integration

Bring Skills Workflow to where your team already talks.

- Talk to Skills Workflow straight from your chat tools — Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, Google Chat.
- Pull reports, approve work, and move jobs forward without leaving the conversation.
- Receive alerts in the chat, the moment they matter.

## Creation

Go from idea to set-up in a fraction of the time.

- Generate whole projects, jobs, and client reports from a short description.
- Auto-assign the right people based on who did the work before.
- Spin up a dashboard from a single sentence, then fine-tune it together with AI.

## Have a request?

If there is a workflow you wish AI already handled, tell your Skills Workflow contact — this roadmap is shaped directly by what agencies ask for.
