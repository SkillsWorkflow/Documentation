A day at your agency, minus the busywork. Here is Skills Workflow AI in the moments that actually eat your team's time — every request made in plain language, right inside the platform you already work in.

## Kick off new work the moment it lands

> *"Create a job for the Northwind spring campaign from this email."*

A client approves scope in an email. Instead of re-keying it into a form, describe it — the work is created, filled in, and ready, and you are only asked for the details that are genuinely missing.

## Turn rough notes into a real brief

> *"Turn these notes into a proper brief."*

Paste the brain-dump. Get back a structured brief that follows your own template — objective, audience, deliverables all in place — ready for the team to build against.

## Ask your data a straight question

> *"How is the Northwind retainer tracking this month?"*

No dashboards to hunt through, no export to a spreadsheet. Ask in plain language and Skills Workflow reads your data and answers you — budget used, what is sitting in approval, what is overdue, who is overloaded. Stop looking around; just ask.

## Polish any text on the spot

> *"Make this sound warmer before it goes to the client."*

Rewrite, shorten, or shift the tone of a brief, a comment, or a client note — without leaving the editor you are already typing in.

## Reshape how work flows

> *"Add a legal review step before Approved."*

Change stages, approvals, and who signs off just by describing the outcome you want. No configuration screens to learn.

## Make the view fit each team

> *"Add a board of this week's deliverables to my workspace."*

Tune the panels and filters on a workspace, or build a brand-new one — shaped around how each team actually works, not how software thinks they should.
